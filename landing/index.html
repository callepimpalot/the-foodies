How it works

The loop

01

Capture

Paste a recipe as text, attach a photo or screenshot, or both at once. It gets pulled into a structured recipe, and before anything saves you can ask for changes in a back-and-forth conversation until it's right.

Text + photo, combined
02

Plan

One meal a day, laid out a few days at a time. Tap a day, pick a saved recipe, mark it as leftovers from another day, or just leave a note. Gaps are fine — it's a plan, not a contract.

03

Shop

Once the plan is locked, the ingredients from every recipe on it get pulled together into one checkable list, consolidated and sorted by category so you're not reading four recipes in the store.

04

Cook

A step-by-step view for when your hands are actually busy — a servings stepper that scales quantities live, and a simple countdown timer built in, so you're not switching apps mid-recipe.

Worth saying plainly

What it isn't

—No accounts

Nobody signs up, nobody signs in. It's just there.

—No subscription

Nothing to renew, nothing that stops working if a card expires.

—No ads

Nothing trying to sell you a different recipe than the one you saved.

—Your own recipes

What's in it is what you or your family cooked — not a catalog to browse.

Most recipe apps make their money on your attention or your card. This one doesn't need to — it just needs to work in the kitchen.
